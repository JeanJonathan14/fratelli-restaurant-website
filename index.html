A different kind of night

Crafted for the evening crowd.

Fratelli brings together the soul of a neighborhood grill house and the polish of a modern dining room — smoky, rich, and entirely memorable. The room glows with low amber light, the grills are always lively, and every table feels like a place where stories can stretch late into the night.

What makes it feel different

Generous plates, honest ingredients, and a rhythm that feels both relaxed and elevated. It is warm without being loud, luxurious without being formal.

House signature

Slow-roasted meats, golden crusts, peppered sauces, and cocktails that settle in just right — made for gathering, lingering, and returning.

Grill house

Roasted goat, pork ribs, charcoal chicken, and steakhouse classics done with depth and swagger.

Atmosphere

Low light, warm textures, and a muted luxury that feels intimate instead of overdesigned.

Late-night bar

Craft cocktails, easy conversation, and the kind of mood that keeps the room alive long after sunset.

The Fratelli way

An unapologetically rich neighborhood ritual.

At Fratelli, we believe the best meals leave more than a satisfied appetite. They create a sense of place. The room is designed to be lived in — a little dramatic, a little smoky, a little indulgent — and full of the kind of details that make people want to return.

From the first charcoal aroma at the entrance to the final conversation over dessert and drinks, every touch is intentional: the colors are warm, the music is deep, and the food is served like a welcome rather than a performance.
